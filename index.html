The method

A simple loop that takes you from "I've seen this" to "I've got this."

  1. 1

    Understand

    Start with a summary or a podcast episode to get the shape of the topic.

  2. 2

    Remember

    Drill the details with flashcards until recall is automatic.

  3. 3

    Check

    Take a quiz to find the gaps while there's still time to fill them.

  4. 4

    Prove it

    Sit a full practice test and earn the confidence you'll bring on exam day.
