Backup & Restore

Your flashcards, quizzes, summaries and scores live only in this browser. Download a backup file now and then, so clearing your history or switching computers never costs you your work.

What's in this browser

    Download a backup

    Saves everything to one small .json file. Keep it somewhere safe, like a cloud drive or email it to yourself.

    Restore from a backup

    Choose a backup file. You'll see what's inside before anything changes.

      Merge

      Adds anything from the backup that's missing here. Nothing you have now is removed or changed. Best scores are kept.

      Replace

      Makes this browser match the backup exactly. Anything not in the backup is removed.
